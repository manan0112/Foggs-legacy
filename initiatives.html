Other FOGGS' Initiatives

Dialogue, research, and engagement across global governance, climate and sustainability, and international cooperation.

Overview

Beyond its flagship strategic projects, FOGGS pursued a wide range of complementary initiatives spanning global governance reform, digital education, development finance, local hubs, and broader partnerships. These included sustained engagement with UN reform processes — from Our Common Agenda roundtables to the Summit of the Future and Think7/T20 tracks; a three-year advisory partnership with GeSI's Digital with Purpose movement on the Education Track of its global summits; contributions to the Fourth Financing for Development Conference in Seville; the sparta.komvos Sparta Office Hub connecting global issues to regional audiences; strategic partnerships with Greycells, MEGA, and the Thabo Mbeki Foundation; a sustained online dialogue series on global governance (Open Consultation Mondays); applied sustainability research and SDG partnerships in India and Greece; and a range of targeted publications and convening activities advancing FOGGS's mission of inclusive multilateralism and a more sustainable world.

UN Reform Engagement

FOGGS engaged systematically with UN reform processes from 2021 to 2024, contributing research, roundtables, and written submissions to key intergovernmental tracks and civil society coalitions.

Our Common Agenda (OCA) Roundtables (2021–2022)

FOGGS engaged extensively with the UN Secretary-General's September 2021 Our Common Agenda (OCA) report. Executive Board member Harris Gleckman authored a background paper on the OCA's governance proposals (Oct 2021) and FOGGS published a critical review of the report (Nov 2021), welcoming its energy while arguing it did not go far enough. FOGGS then convened two focused roundtables: on 27 January 2022, a discussion with senior officials from Permanent Missions to the UN and think tanks held under the Chatham House Rule; and on 18 February 2022, a second closed roundtable with non-state actors including science organisations, think tanks, local authorities, and the private sector.

Summit of the Future Engagement (2023–2024)

FOGGS participated throughout the intergovernmental process leading to the UN Summit of the Future (22–23 September 2024). Written contributions included a submission on the Emergency Platform proposal (March 2023), a comprehensive written submission (Dec 2023), and a response to Rev.1 of the Pact for the Future (May 2024). FOGGS Executive Director Georgios Kostakos attended the Summit and wrote A Pact for Humanity's Future that Remains Precarious. A post-SOTF UN Day event (24 October 2024) produced a recording and a presentation on next steps.

Coalition for the UN We Need (C4UN) and Global Futures Forum (2022–2023)

FOGGS joined the Coalition for the UN We Need (C4UN) as a partner in 2021. FOGGS co-organised the online Europe Regional Futures Forum (13 March 2023) and FOGGS Executive Director Georgios Kostakos co-facilitated the Development track at the civil society-led Global Futures Forum (GFF) (20–21 March 2023, New York). The process produced the Interim People's Pact for the Future (May 2023), incorporating the FOGGS Global Resilience Council proposal on page 7. FOGGS also participated in the monthly OCA Discussion Series co-hosted by C4UN and the Baha'i International Community throughout 2022.

Think7 / T20 Engagement (2021–2022)

FOGGS President Richard Kinley was a speaker at the T20 Forum on Climate Change (10 May 2021, Italian G20 Presidency). FOGGS Executive Director Georgios Kostakos participated in the Think7 Inception Conference (24–26 January 2022) and actively joined the T7 Summit in Berlin (23–24 May 2022), where the T7 Communiqué referenced the Global Resilience Council on page 10. Kostakos and Harris Gleckman also co-authored a T7 policy brief on the GRC published under the International Cooperation for the Global Common Good task force.

ACUNS Institutional Membership (2021–2024)

FOGGS joined the Academic Council on the United Nations System (ACUNS) as an institutional member in 2021. At the ACUNS Annual Meeting 2023 in Washington, D.C., FOGGS Executive Director Georgios Kostakos chaired a roundtable on the PRG and Russia–Ukraine war; Cilene Victor spoke on Global South perspectives; and Paola Bettelli and Tapio Kanninen participated across multiple panels. At the ACUNS Annual Meeting 2024 at the UN University in Tokyo, FOGGS organised three roundtables on the Global Resilience Council, institutional capacities in a changing world, and the UN Secretariat's contemporary role. On the sidelines, FOGGS reached an understanding on cooperation with the Kyoto Peacebuilding Center (KPC).

Digital with Purpose — Education Track Advisory (2022–2024)

Over three consecutive years, FOGGS held an advisory and content partnership with GeSI's Digital with Purpose (DwP) Movement on the Education Track of the DwP Global Summits. At the 2022 Summit (Lisbon), Executive Board Member Veerle Vandeweerd chaired a session on education in a rapidly changing digital world; Georgios Kostakos presented COVIDEA alongside Stephen Downes. At the 2023 Summit (Lisbon), Vandeweerd presented both DEMOCRAT and COVIDEA. At the 2024 Summit (9–11 July, Cascais, Portugal), FOGGS co-convened the Education Track: Veerle Vandeweerd and Georgios Kostakos led the track while FOGGS Associate Manan Shah chaired the Education opening plenary, bringing together participants from government, education, gaming, ed-tech, and IT sectors. Selected session recordings are available through the DwP recorded events archive.

Fourth Financing for Development Conference (FFD4) Engagement (2025)

In preparation for the 4th International Conference on Financing for Development (FFD4), FOGGS Executive Director Georgios Kostakos joined the UN Townhall convened by the FFD4 Co-facilitators (7 February 2025). His written intervention urged a shift toward self-empowerment, human security, and local economies. FOGGS was represented at FFD4 in Seville, Spain (30 June–3 July 2025) by Georgios Kostakos and GSPN partners, distributing Reimagining Development from the Ground Up, a handout advocating a development paradigm rooted in local capacities and resilience.

Open Consultation Mondays

Open Consultation Mondays was an online dialogue platform bringing together experts, policymakers, researchers, and civil society representatives for regular discussion of pressing global governance challenges. Launched amid rising geopolitical tension, climate crises, economic instability, and technological disruption, the series responded to calls for reform of global governance structures.

Each session focused on a theme tied to global governance or multilateral institutions, prioritising open participation and active discussion over formal presentations. Topics ranged from UN reform and geopolitical shifts to climate governance, development finance, the roles of middle powers and civil society, and the influence of narratives and leadership on international politics.

Climate and Sustainability Initiatives

FOGGS engaged in applied research and partnerships advancing sustainable development and the implementation of the UN's Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), with projects in India and Greece.

SDGs in India

In collaboration with Indian research partners, FOGGS produced a report examining the implementation of the SDG framework in India, identifying structural challenges, policy gaps, and opportunities for stronger alignment between national development priorities and global sustainability goals. The 2021 report, SDGs in India, offers analysis of SDG progress and recommends pathways for accelerating delivery in key areas including education, health, gender equality, and climate action.

Hellenic Forum for Sustainable Development (ELFOVA)

FOGGS co-founded and supported the Hellenic Forum for Sustainable Development (ELFOVA), a national platform promoting sustainable development policies and practices in Greece. Operating in partnership with ELIAMEP (the Hellenic Foundation for European and Foreign Policy), ELFOVA worked to map SDG implementation in Greece, foster dialogue among public, private, and civil society stakeholders, and strengthen Greece's contribution to the global sustainability agenda. The project's research outcomes are available through the University of Athens EEEP programme.

sparta.komvos (2024–2025)

The FOGGS Executive Director Sparta office (sparta.komvos), inaugurated in March 2024, served as a local hub connecting global issues to regional audiences. It hosted preparatory meetings for the Heyday Road (May 2024) — a peace-and-sport initiative carrying the message of the Olympic Truce from Sparta to Olympia — and organised a Review of International Developments and in-person talks on AI, European futures, and sustainability.

Partnerships and Networks

Greycells / GreyTalks Partnership

FOGGS maintained an ongoing partnership with Greycells — Association of Former International Civil Servants for Development, promoting their GreyTalks dialogue series. FOGGS Executive Director Georgios Kostakos was a speaker at the GreyTalk "The Case for a Revision of the UN Charter" (28 March 2023), alongside Augusto López Claros and Maja Groff. FOGGS, Greycells, and Peace Child International jointly organised a briefing for Geneva Permanent Missions and NGOs (8 June 2023) on civil society proposals for the UN Summits 2023–2025.

MEGA — Mobilizing an Earth Governance Alliance

In December 2023, FOGGS joined MEGA — Mobilizing an Earth Governance Alliance as a co-sponsoring organisation, a coalition of civil society organisations working with like-minded governments to strengthen environmental governance.

Thabo Mbeki Foundation Consultations (2024)

In March 2024, FOGGS Executive Director Georgios Kostakos, together with partners from the Inclusive Society Institute (ISI) — Buyelwa Sonjica and Daryl Swanepoel — held consultations with the Thabo Mbeki Foundation in Johannesburg, including a meeting with former South African President Thabo Mbeki, on cooperation around global governance reform and the Global Resilience Council.

Other Activities

  • Running Out of Diplomats (April 2024) — FOGGS published Running out of Diplomats? Exploring the drivers of the global 'Diplomatic Shortage' by Elisabeth Clemmons and Fotini Zarogianni, and convened an online event (24 April 2024) with the authors alongside Markus Kornprobst (Vienna School of International Studies), moderated by Manan Shah (recording).
  • ERC Appointment Open Letter (2024) — FOGGS partnered with UNA-UK and the Blue Smoke project to facilitate a letter to UN Secretary-General Guterres signed by 60+ former UN officials and civil society leaders, calling for a merit-based process for appointing the UN's next Emergency Relief Coordinator and OCHA Head.
  • Declaration Supporting UNSG Guterres (October 2023) — More than 300 former UN system staff signed a declaration supporting UNSG Guterres for his objective comments on the Middle East situation, an initiative facilitated by FOGGS and referenced in PassBlue's November 2023 UN roundup.
  • Delphi Economic Forum (April 2022) — FOGGS was a content partner at the Delphi Economic Forum (6–9 April, Delphi, Greece), co-sponsoring a panel on holistic approaches to non-military threats alongside the Global Military Advisory Council on Climate Change, moderated by Georgios Kostakos with keynote speakers Dhanasree Jayaram and Olivia Lazard (recording).

Impact

The Open Consultation Mondays series broadened dialogue on the future of global governance and multilateral cooperation, surfacing diverse perspectives and encouraging reflection on reforms and innovations to strengthen global institutions. FOGGS's sustained engagement with UN reform processes — from the Our Common Agenda roundtables and Summit of the Future submissions to Think7/T20 participation and ACUNS institutional membership — helped shape intergovernmental discourse and ensured civil society voices reached high-level deliberations. The Digital with Purpose education advisory partnership advanced practical conversations on digital learning and inclusion across three consecutive global summits. The sparta.komvos Sparta Office Hub created a bridge between global governance debates and regional audiences in the Peloponnese. FOGGS's engagement with FFD4, partnerships with Greycells, MEGA, and the Thabo Mbeki Foundation, and targeted publications on diplomatic capacity, emergency relief, and peace extended the organisation's reach and contributed to multistakeholder conversations on the future of international cooperation. The climate and sustainability research partnerships contributed applied analysis to ongoing SDG implementation debates, bridging global frameworks and national-level action in India and Greece.
